About Me

Initially started with coding courses at UCSD in 2025, after looking for work in software engineering I decided to pick up another skill that AI has ran rampant in and began art. I have been starting from the ground up to learn as much as I can at a time so that I can create acceptable works that clients would be happy to recieve. Most of my learning has been through a private teacher as well as Colosso courses. Feedback is precious in art, it allows me to understand where I had fallen short and what is needed in order to go beyond your current skill level.
